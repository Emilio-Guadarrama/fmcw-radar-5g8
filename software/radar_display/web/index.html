Targets from decision 002. They are assumptions and have not been demonstrated. A simulation on this PC cannot pass them.

FMCW
The radar sends a tone whose frequency rises steadily, and listens while sending.
Chirp / sweep
One rise in frequency. Its width is the bandwidth.
Beat signal
The difference between what is sent now and the returning echo. A farther object gives a higher beat frequency.
Range spectrum
The beat signal sorted by frequency (an FFT), relabelled as distance.
Range cell
The spacing of the distance grid, c/(2B). It is not the same as the closest two targets that can be told apart.
Window
A weighting of each chirp. Hann hides false side bumps but widens peaks. Rectangular gives narrower peaks with bigger side bumps.
Frame
A group of chirps processed into one screen update.
dB, dBV
A ratio scale: +20 dB is 10 times the voltage. dBV compares with 1 volt peak.
SNR
Echo power compared with noise power, after processing.
Threshold
The height an echo must pass to count. It is set from the measured noise.
False alarm
Noise passing the threshold and looking like a target.
